# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want Bitcoin Lifeboat to be a useful, serious project for people who care
about self-custody recovery. Contributors are expected to make participation
open and respectful regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Focus disagreement on the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility when you make a mistake.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language or imagery where it is not directly relevant to the work.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that maintainers reasonably judge inappropriate for the project.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when a person represents the project in
public spaces, including GitHub, issue trackers, chat rooms, events, email, and
social media.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers through the security contact in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) if privacy is needed. Maintainers may remove
comments, close issues, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation,
or permanently ban participants when necessary.
