# Recovery Day Press Kit

Use this page when announcing a local Bitcoin Recovery Day meetup, workshop, or
family drill. Keep public claims modest: the event helps people rehearse, find
missing recovery material, and leave with a private action list.

## One-Sentence Description

Bitcoin Recovery Day is an annual self-custody practice day where Bitcoin users
test their recovery plan with watch-only metadata, test wallets, public-safe
reports, and printed runbooks.

## Short Description

Bitcoin Recovery Day turns wallet recovery into a yearly practice habit. In a
local workshop, participants learn what a descriptor is, how to verify a
Lifeboat download, how to run a Readiness Check with watch-only metadata, and how
to write down gaps they should fix privately.

No one should bring seed words, passphrases, or private keys to a public event.

## Project Boilerplate

Bitcoin Lifeboat is not a wallet, not a custody service, not a seed phrase
manager, not an inheritance legal service, and not a recovery company. It is a
free, open-source diagnostic tool that helps you test whether your recovery plan
works.

## Event Listing Copy

Bring a laptop and, if you already know how to export it, a watch-only wallet
descriptor or public wallet export. Do not bring seed words or passphrases. The
workshop will use sample descriptors for the public demo, then give attendees
quiet time to run their own checks privately.

Attendees should leave with a short action list: what metadata is missing, what
runbook fields need work, what family or signer drill should be repeated, and
when the next drill should happen.

## Image Guidance

Use images that show practice materials, checklists, a projector with sample
descriptors, or people working privately at laptops. Do not show seed words,
passphrases, real wallet balances, QR codes from participant wallets, or close-up
photos of hardware-wallet screens.

## FAQ

### Is this a recovery service?

No. Bitcoin Lifeboat does not take custody, hold keys, recover funds for users,
or provide legal inheritance advice.

### Should people bring real seed phrases?

No. Real seed phrases, passphrases, xprvs, private keys, and wallet passwords
should not enter the event room workflow.

### What should a meetup publish afterward?

Publish general lessons, attendance, and links to the public kit. Do not publish
participant wallet types, descriptors, addresses, reports, runbooks, photos of
screens, or names tied to recovery details.
